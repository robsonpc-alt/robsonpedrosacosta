Academic profile

Robson Pedrosa Costa is a historian specializing in slavery, gender, and the Atlantic World, with particular interests in Brazil, Africa, and the African diaspora.

His current research focuses especially on the histories and experiences of African and Afro-descendant women, examining slavery, resistance, freedom, motherhood, family, mobility, religion, and the multiple connections linking Africa and the diaspora.

His broader scholarship also addresses institutional slavery, religion, paternalism, emancipation, and slave-owning slaves in Africa and Brazil.

He is the author of Paternalism, Transgression and Slave Resistance in Brazil (De Gruyter Oldenbourg, 2022). He was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Lisbon (2022–2024) and currently leads collaborative international research on women, slavery, Africa, and the African diaspora.
